About

Why TEasy exists

Most small businesses in India don't have a full-time accountant on staff. What they have is a shoebox of bills, a Tally file, and someone — an owner, a part-time bookkeeper, an overworked accountant handling ten clients at once — retyping every single line item by hand every month.

TEasy reads the bill for you. Photograph a purchase bill, a sales invoice, or a bank statement, and it comes back as a structured, GST-correct entry you glance at and approve, then push straight into Tally Prime. What used to take an evening takes a few minutes.
